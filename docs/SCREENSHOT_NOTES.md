# Screenshot Notes

The images in `../images/` show the **Kunooze Library** reader interface at a mobile-sized viewport. They are included to explain the public reader experience: the Arabic right-to-left home screen, academic catalogue, and student guide.

The academic labels and counts visible in the screenshots are illustrative interface data. Live materials, announcements, stages, semesters, subjects, and PDFs are controlled by the library owner and can change after publication. The screenshots do not contain student records, private credentials, publisher functionality, or unpublished resources.
